Basic Structure of a Paragraph

• Topic Sentence: Usually the first sentence. It introduces the main idea or central topic.
• Body / Supporting Sentences: The middle sentences. They provide facts, examples, explanations, or evidence to develop the main idea.
• Concluding Sentence: The final sentence. It summarizes the main point or provides a smooth transition to the next paragraph
